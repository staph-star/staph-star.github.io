# Privacy Policy — FieldAssist: Baseball & Softball Defense

- **Developer:** PlayerAssist
- **Package / Bundle ID:** `com.playerassist.fieldassist`
- **Effective Date:** October 28, 2026

**PlayerAssist** ("we", "our", or "us") built the **FieldAssist: Baseball & Softball Defense** mobile and web application (`com.playerassist.fieldassist`). We respect your privacy and designed FieldAssist to operate locally on your device without collecting, selling, or tracking your personal information.

## 1. Information We Collect
- **No Personal Data Collection:** FieldAssist does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email address, phone number, or location to use the application.
- **Local Device Storage Only:** Your selected sport (Baseball or Fastpitch Softball), age division, visual overlays, saved custom scenarios, and unlock status are stored locally on your device using standard local storage (`localStorage`). This data never leaves your device.
- **No Advertising or Third-Party Ad Tracking:** FieldAssist contains zero third-party advertisements and does not access or use your device's Advertising ID.

## 2. In-App Purchases & Promo Codes
- **Google Play / Apple App Store Billing:** Optional one-time tier upgrades (such as Player Pro Edition and Coach Edition) are processed securely through Google Play Billing or the Apple App Store (and verified via RevenueCat using anonymous installation identifiers). We never receive or store your credit card or payment details.
- **Optional Promo Code Redemption:** If you redeem a promotional or team access code, the application sends only the promo code string and a non-reversible anonymous device hash to verify single-use code validity. No personally identifiable information is transmitted.

## 3. Sharing & Exporting Play Cards
When using the optional "Share Play Card" feature, the PNG image is generated locally on your device and handed directly to your operating system's native share sheet. FieldAssist does not upload your exported play cards to any external server.

## 4. Children's Privacy
FieldAssist is an educational sports coaching and defensive positioning reference tool intended for coaches, parents, and players. Because the application does not collect, store, or share any personal information from any user of any age, it complies with applicable children's and family privacy standards.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
